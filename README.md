# Job Estimator

Take a couple of photos of a room, or the plans, say what the job is, and get a full
material takeoff, labor, and a priced estimate, with current lumber-yard prices.
Snap a picture and get a before/after preview of what the finished job will look like.

Made by R.E. Buotte Construction. Uses Claude by Anthropic: you need your own
Claude API key from [platform.claude.com](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys).

## Mac

**[Download Job Estimator for Mac](https://github.com/Rbuilder1/job-estimator-download/releases/latest/download/JobEstimator-mac.dmg)**
(Macs with Apple Silicon: M1 or newer)

1. Open the downloaded **JobEstimator-mac.dmg** and drag **Job Estimator** into **Applications**.
2. Open Job Estimator from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the
   app, because it isn't from the App Store. Click **Done**, then go to **System Settings →
   Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway** next to Job Estimator, and confirm.
   You only do this once.
3. In **⚙ Settings**, paste your Claude API key. It's kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Estimates work anywhere with an internet connection. The before/after previews are made
by an image generator on the Windows PC that has it set up: on the same Wi-Fi, enter that
PC's address and link code in ⚙ Settings.
